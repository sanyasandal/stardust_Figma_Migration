The Fortian Digitised

The Fortian has been chronicling the lives and achievements of students and staff since 1899. A project to digitise The Fortian, nearing completion, will make this wonderful resource available online. Editions from the nineteenth century and the First World War period, plus recent editions, are available below.

Research Tip – If you are searching for a person or an event, most programs allow you to search specific words in each edition of The Fortian to easily find the name or special occasion.
