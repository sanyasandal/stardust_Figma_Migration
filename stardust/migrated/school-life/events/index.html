Events

School events bring learning to life and help shape our school culture. They help students build confidence, connect with others and celebrate their progress. Events also give families a chance to be involved in school life and share in their child’s achievements.

Our school event may include:

  • school orientation days
  • sports carnivals
  • open day and school tours
  • Harmony Week and Book Week events
  • excursions and incursions
  • performances, concerts or school productions
  • fundraising, charity and community events
  • award assemblies
  • graduation and other celebrations.

Contact us to learn more about our events.

Catchup Photographs Day

For students absent on School Photographs Day, this will be the final opportunity to have their individual photo taken.

Friday 26 February; commencing at 10:00am

Speech Day at the State Theatre

The annual school Speech Day is a compulsory event for all students. Students must be in full school uniform at this event which includes the school tie. A permission to attend note will be sent to all parents/carers. Year 7 students make their way to State Theatre and released at the end of Speech Day, around 1:00pm. Roll Call will occur before students enter State Theatre. Parents will receive a text message if their child is unexplained absence or have arrived late. Students who require teacher supervision will be escorted back to school until 3.15pm.
