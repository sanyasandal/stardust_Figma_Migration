Fort Fest is on Friday 18 September, 3:15pm - 8:30pm.

Fort Fest is an evening event for all the school and local community, with music, food, games and stalls. Run jointly by the SRC and P&C as a fundraiser, it is also a great way to meet and connect with other Fort Street families.
