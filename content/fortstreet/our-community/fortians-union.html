The Fortians Union

The Fortian alumni associations began in 1894 to:

1. Build a Strong Community:

  • Create a place where members can connect, have fun, and support each other.

2. Give Back to Fort Street:

  • Actively contribute to the success of Fort Street High School through volunteer work, mentorship, or other initiatives.

3. Celebrate Our Legacy:

  • Honour the rich history and traditions of Fort Street while embracing innovation and progress.

For enquiries or to get in touch with the Fortians Union, please contact us via email at: fortians@fortians.au
